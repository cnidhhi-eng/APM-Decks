District SmartFilter
Context-aware
event discovery

District lists hundreds of events — but treats a solo night out, a family outing, and a girls’ trip to a café all the same. It shouldn’t.

By Nidhi Chauhan · District APM Application · May 2026

The Gap

Great listings. Zero context.

District does discovery well — but discovery without context is just noise. When I open the app to find something to do with my 10-year-old cousin, I get the same view as when I’m scouting for a girls’ night out.

The Solution

SmartFilter — who you’re with shapes what you see

SmartFilter adds two new dimensions to District’s discovery layer: context (who am I going with?) and constraints (age, vibe, safety). Instead of just Dining, Movies, Events, users get filters that match their actual needs.

1
Context layer

Who am I going with?

A one-tap group selector at the top of the discovery screen: Solo · With kids · Girls trip · Date · Mixed group. This single input immediately re-ranks the Events feed based on suitability.

2
Age filter

Age-appropriate tagging

Events get tagged at the listing level: All ages · 13+ · 18+ · 21+. This data already exists (venue licensing, age-restricted events). SmartFilter surfaces it contextually.

3
Vibe tags

Aesthetic & safety signals

“Aesthetic” is real user language — District should speak it. Vibe tags like Aesthetic · Low-key · Lively · Outdoor · Well-lit surface the qualitative feel of a venue.

4
Saved contexts

Remember my usual crew

Users save their common contexts: “Weekend with family” or “Girls night.” Next time they open Events, one tap loads their preferred filter set and ordering logic.

Success Metrics

How I’d measure if this works

↑
Filter usage rate
% of sessions where a context or age filter is applied. Baseline: near zero. Target: 40%+ within 60 days of launch.
↑
Click-through rate
Filtered feed CTR vs. default feed. Hypothesis: contextual results drive 2x higher engagement per listing shown.
↓
Drop-off on discovery
% of users who open Events but tap nothing. Current: high. SmartFilter should reduce this meaningfully.
↑
Saved contexts
Users who save a context (e.g. “With family”) show intent to return. Strong retention signal beyond bookings.
🎯
North Star
Relevant results on first scroll

The goal isn’t more filters — it’s fewer irrelevant results. If a parent with a young kid opens Events and the first 5 listings are appropriate for their context, we’ve won.

Roadmap

Phased rollout

Phase 1 · 2 weeks
Age tagging
  • Tag existing listings: All ages / 13+ / 18+ / 21+
  • Simple age filter on Events
  • Hide age-restricted events when filter active
  • No ML required — rule-based
Phase 2 · 4 weeks
Context modes
  • Group selector: Solo / Kids / Girls / Date / Mixed
  • Re-rank feed by context rules
  • A/B test vs. default feed
  • Measure CTR & session depth
Phase 3 · 8 weeks
Vibe tags
  • Aesthetic / Low-key / Lively / Outdoor tags
  • “Reviewed by women” badge from rating data
  • Vibe filter layer in Events
  • Surface in event detail pages
Phase 4 · 12 weeks
Saved contexts
  • Save up to 3 named contexts per user
  • One-tap load on app open
  • Personalisation signals feed recommendation model
  • National rollout if P2/P3 KPIs hold
